Numbers, months and feelings, translated both ways.

    In Spanish, months are written in lowercase: en marzo. Dates read day first: el 5 de mayo.

      Most feelings use estar or sentirse: Estoy cansado / Me siento cansado. Adjectives change with gender: cansado (m), cansada (f).

        Food words vary by country. Where Spain and Latin America differ, the note says which is which.

          Spanish opens questions with ¿ and exclamations with ¡. Tú is informal and usted is formal; phrases below are informal unless noted.
